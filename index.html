中文

本应用是一个新闻阅读器，用于阅读公开网站 cnbeta.com.tw 上的文章和评论。

我们不收集任何个人信息。

  • 应用没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录。
  • 应用不包含任何第三方统计、广告或追踪工具，也不会跨应用或跨网站追踪你。
  • 你的已读记录、字体大小和外观设置只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传，也不会发送给我们或任何第三方。

网络请求

为了显示内容，应用会直接从 cnbeta.com.tw 及其图片服务器读取文章列表、正文、图片和评论。这些请求由你的设备直接发出，对方网站可能按其自身的隐私政策记录访问信息（例如 IP 地址）。我们不经手也无法访问这些数据。

发表评论

点击"发表评论"会在应用内打开 cnbeta.com.tw 的网页，登录、验证码和评论内容都由该网站处理，请参阅该网站的隐私政策。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的信息。

政策变更

如本政策有变更，会更新本页面并修改上方日期。

联系方式

English

This app is a news reader for articles and comments published on the public website cnbeta.com.tw.

We do not collect any personal information.

  • There are no accounts; no sign-up or login is required.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • Your read history, font size and appearance settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to any third party.

Network requests

To show content, the app loads article lists, article text, images and comments directly from cnbeta.com.tw and its image servers. These requests are made by your device, and that website may log access information (such as your IP address) under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or have access to this data.

Posting comments

Tapping "Write a comment" opens the cnbeta.com.tw website inside the app. Sign-in, captcha and comment content are handled by that website; please see its privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
